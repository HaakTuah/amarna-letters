## Readings taken

- **Arwad's cisterns count from the siege's start,** not from a blockade: Strabo says it had water of its own, so it is
  no longer a city whose water comes by boat (the "Island" kind, Tyre's). An island whose cisterns count only from a
  blockade would be a new kind; the nearer kind is taken (principle: "Different data, same rules", no new rule for one
  city).
- **"Where he served" is the seat of his court or his post:** the sources place Pharaoh's officials and envoys at his
  court, which sat at Akhetaten in 1346.
- **The middle is the median** where estimates cluster (density), and the middle of the range otherwise.
- **Game-mechanic readings graded [G]** in the dossiers (how a rule works) are not estimates of the past, so they are
  out of this rule's reach.
