## What changed

- **`wordHomeAt(s, map, court, province, turn, extra = 0)`** (`rules/court.ts`, exported from the engine): the season
  word of a province's season reaches a court, by the couriers' road home from the place to the court in that season:
  the same season where they make it within the season, else one season more than `courierSeasons`, plus `extra`;
  Infinity where no road leads. Roads differ by direction (boats go downstream only, winter is read at the
  destination), and word home takes the road home (round 1).
- **Its readers:** `sight` (what a court sees as current: word comes within the season), `knownHolders` and
  `siegesKnown` (`rules/known-land.ts`), the notices' `TurnEngine.heardAt` (`turn/moods.ts`), a caravan's setting out
  (`setOutHeard`, `turn/couriers.ts`), `wordCame` (`rules/caravans-known.ts`) and a census's count (`turn/counts.ts`).
  The reports change clock (from `wordSeasons`); the notices, caravans, units, census and sight change only where the
  road home differs from the road out (`heardAt` read the road out before), and the census comes home a season later
  from far off, with the province's report. A notice with no road home is still delivered as one from near; a report
  from there never comes (no such province on the default map); a census with none still takes four seasons.
- **Near or far, for news, by the road home** (`TurnEngine.wordFrom`, round 2): whether word from a province comes
  within the season decides what a court is told at once and what it sees as it is: a province lost (`setOwner`), siege
  lines, market and works notices, trade's besieged notice, a live caravan, an own unit out of sight (`reportOf`,
  `shownAt`, `orderedUnit`), and the men a levy is checked against (round 3). `wordTo`, the road out, stays for orders going out (`sendOrders`, scheduled orders) and
  loads carried (gold home, loot, a harvest's books).
- **Word kept as it came** (`seeLand`, `turn/travel.ts`): each season a court keeps the word that has come home from each
  province (`GameState.known`), and `knownHolders` and `siegesKnown` read it, since the clock reads today's roads and
  relay stations: roads cut or stations razed later take back nothing heard (Codex, round 1). `knownHolders` looks back
  as far as the game goes, not six seasons, so a road home of six seasons or more still brings word (Codex, round 1).
- **For the news lane** (#618, #601): `wordHomeAt` is the clock for any news brought home from a province.
- **Rulebook** (`llm/src/rules-travel.ts`): news of distant land comes at the couriers' pace with the news told from
  there; a kingdom's fall goes from court to court by the fastest road. **Design notes** 13 and 35. **Save version 57.**
- **Tests:** a far levy that failed, season by season: its notice and Nubia's report of that season reach the court in
  the same season, failing with the old clock; a pair whose road home is slower than the road out (Hattusa to a court at
  Akhetaten), failing with the road out; an own unit at Hattusa shown at its last reported place, near by the road
  out and a season away by the road home, failing with the road out; word of Babylon heard at Hattusa still heard after every road and relay station
is cut, failing without the kept word (`levy-word.test.ts`). The far-levy twin's `heard` needs no hedge for whichever
  came first. The canary's pick of a far province reads the same clock.
