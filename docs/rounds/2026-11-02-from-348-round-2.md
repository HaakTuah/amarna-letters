The second review round of item 17+ (#348) left points outside 17++'s text for a side lane (`docs/BUILD-PROGRESS.md`,

## Built

- **(c) Whom a court gives a contested name to reads the claims as it knows them.** `nameGivenBy` (`power.ts`), lacking
  a greeting of the court's own, gave the name to the power whose own land it is only if he truly claimed nothing else
  (`!claimed.has(p)`), so a claim the court had never heard of changed what its view and letters called him. A claimant
  known to the court by his own land's name is one it has heard claim nothing else (`claimHeard` keeps no entry for a
  claim of one's own land), so the check is now that alone. Test: Byblos claims Amurru's name out of Egypt's hearing,
  and Egypt still calls him Gubla and gives Gubla's name to him (`names-as-claims.test.ts`; it failed before the fix).
- **(d) The spectator's record of contested names, unused, is removed.** `nameGivenBy` and `landFor` were only ever
  called with a court; without one (notices, logs, god view) `land` names each power by its own land. The branch giving
  a name to the first claimant, and the claim's season it read, are gone; `nameGivenBy`, `landFor`, `knownName`,
  `claimantsOf` and `contestedNames` now all take a court (from Claude's review: the last three still kept a world's
  record that only two tests asked for, now asking a court).
- **(e) 17+'s readings corrected:** 29 said the engine used a world's record and 33 that its notices did, against 30
  (and the code), where the engine names each power by its own land; 33 stood after 34 and 35. Both now say what is
  built, and 33 is back in order (`docs/rounds/2026-11-01-opening-as-it-stood.md`). The design note says the same
  (`docs/design/46-the-opening-as-it-stood.md`).
- **(f) The Court section's upkeep:** one drafted upkeep order greyed out both halves (pay and food) for all the troops
  and every province's choice. Now each half is drafted, in a season, either for all the troops or province by province,
  each province once; the other half stays free.
- **(g) A spy caught with gold to stir a province** kept it in his purse counted as skimmed, so his wealth line called
  him a thief. It now counts as `Carried`, gold kept from an errand, whose label reads "gold kept on dismissal or
  capture" (`intel.ts`, `wealth.ts`, the batch summary). The same holds for a spy dismissed on the road home, or whose
  master fell, who keeps what he did not spend (`spyComesHome`, from Claude's review). Two tests in
  `free-cities.test.ts`, each failing before its fix. The same gold left with a spy no longer on his errand
  (`unrest.ts`) is still counted as skimmed: that file is 17++'s.
- **(h) The no-op `CityStarts` entry** (Amurru's army in Amurru, its own city) is gone, and with it the table's `fleet`
  option, which no entry used (`map.ts`); the comment says why Amurru needs no entry.
