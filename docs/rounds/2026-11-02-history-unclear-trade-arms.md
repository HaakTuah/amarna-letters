## What the game rests on, now sourced

- **The 3% start toll**, which the check flagged: Veenhof (*Law and Trade*, 2020, open access): "ca. 3 %", "four pounds
  of tin per standard donkey-load of 130 pounds". The number stands.
- **One kor of barley for a shekel** (the Laws of Eshnunna §1), Engels's rations, Stos-Gale's copper shares.
- **The donkey's load,** 70-90 kg, a [G] estimate between Veenhof's and Engels's figures.

## Facts that changed

- The king's merchants traded "with the palace's goods", not "on his credit" (Kelder and Cline 2018, p. 25).
- Mursili II led "the campaigns of his *Annals*", not "every year" of them (unchecked).
- Kush and Irem "sent Egypt gifts and chiefs' sons"; no war on Irem under the 18th Dynasty is recorded. The Nile door's
  "man of Irem" needs no change.
- A doubt added on the gold deben's weight (Klemm and Klemm) and on the even east-west tin split (Powell et al. 2022:
  southern Iraq after 1500 "relied mainly on the reuse and recycling of bronze"), [C]; the game's shares are proportions
  and stay.

No number the game uses changes: no save version, no seed moves.
