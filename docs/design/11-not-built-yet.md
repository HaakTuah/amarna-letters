## Not built yet

Every decided direction that is not built, one line each, with where it is decided. A quoted title is an entry
of the design document, in whichever tab its system is (`grep -rn "Title" docs/intent` finds it); "question N" is in
`docs/rounds/2026-10-18-sleep-and-simulation.md`; "item N" is in its Build order; "the round report" is the
simulation-fixes round's report, `docs/archive/rounds/2026-10-19-simulation-fixes.md`.

- **Sharing news**: attaching a copy of a report to an envoy ("Information as a structured asset"; items 8 and 11).
  News of who holds what travels since item 7b ([travel and news](13-travel-and-news.md)); news is sold and bought since
  item 11b ([roads and reputations](23-roads-and-reputations.md)).
- **Other courts' own calendars and regnal years**: season names for the courts that have only ordinals today
  ("Season names"), and regnal or limmu years (question 26, plan steps 5-7). The one date label with the
  year BCE and the ending line are built (see "The web map and the interface").
- **The map's later layers**: generals leading armies, drawn on the map (roads by quality, relay stations, walls and
  the fog of war for ownership are drawn: [the living map](43-the-living-map.md)).
- **Errands item 10 left for later** ("Errands, not roles"): carrying a candidate an offer (item 11's live pool), carrying a
  ransom or a bribe by hand, a king's messenger who only delivers (envoys carry letters; since item 11b spies' reports need
  carriers, see [roads and reputations](23-roads-and-reputations.md)), and chaining several stops in one errand (taking
  command is built since item 13, [command at a distance](35-command-at-a-distance.md)). Characters answering questioning, and choosing to lie, themselves rather than by formula.
- **Tablets that need a reader**: built in item 12 ([languages](30-languages.md)), except a tablet sent to a third court
  to be read and its reading sent back ("Tablets are objects in a place"; a copy can be shown abroad by envoy, but no
  translation comes home). The archive goes with the court since item 13.
- **Call size, what 6b left**: a "since you last acted" view of the map (the view stays whole; only the digest of slept
  seasons is shortened, see "Call size" in `04-models.md`), sharing the schema's repeated shapes, and batch processing
  for unattended games ("Output is the other half"); standing answers for characters (item 6b's report; the screen for a
  human seat's standing answers is built since item 8, see [structured press](14-structured-press.md)).
- **Script kings selling or faking news**: they pass on news in clauses (troops seen, a king's strength) but sell none
  and invent none; since item 11b free characters sell news and script kings buy it. Carriers reading, selling or
  swapping what they carry (hiring bands as soldiers is built since item 13).
- **A cheaper summariser**, measured on the same games before it is used ("A cheaper model for condensing,
  to be measured"; the round report, Part E).
- **What item 13 left** ([command at a distance](35-command-at-a-distance.md)): commanders and governors deciding by
  model rather than by script; a risen governor's seat played by a model (it is a script seat, [37](37-answers-to-13.md)).
- **The message-kind fallback for reopening orders**, if kings reopen too often after audiences: reopening offered
  only for a coalition or alliance tablet, evidence of a broken tablet, a ransom demand, or a threat or news of war,
  sorted by a small model that errs toward offering, for model seats only ("If kings reopen almost every
  time, the kind of message decides"; `docs/archive/rounds/ROUND-PROMPT-ADDITIONS.md`, sections 4 and 6.2; Build order "Not yet in
  the order", after item 4; the round report, Part H).
- **Who receives envoys, later candidates**: a minister who receives routine envoys by the king's standing
  instructions, an attention limit the same for every seat, and tablets counting as pressing only where the
  instructions do not cover them; they need a decision first ("Under consideration: standing instructions for
  audiences" and "What the replay showed"; Build order "Not yet in the order"; the round report, "Later candidates").
- **Push notifications** when an envoy arrives ("Platforms"); the web app installs and starts from cache.
- **"A new Idrimi" from an outlaw band**: built for a governor rising to rule his province (item 14, on item 13+'s seats,
  [legends](38-legends.md)); a band taking a city rises with item 17's risen seats and reads the same way.
- **Still to be decided before they are built**: the spec of the economy's later phases, and multiplayer, stages 2
  and 3 (Build order "Not yet in the order"; "Path to multiplayer"). Distance-based travel and interception were built in
  item 7b; the economy's first phase (item 9+) and the royal council (item 16) have been decided and are in the build order.
