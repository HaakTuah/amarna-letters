# 16. The kings who followed

For item 17b: a dead king's heir is the sources' named heir where known, then each next name below, as far as the
record goes; past it a son or brother is drawn. Officials, usurpers and conquerors count as "the next name" (marked).
Each name: grade, reign (BCE, about: [README](README.md#readme-chronology)), relation to the one before, and a trait
only where deeds show the man; "then [S]" where the record stops. Who ruled in 1346: [01-politics.md](01-politics.md).

### The Great Kings

- <a id="16-line-egypt"></a>**Egypt** (after Akhenaten; the disputed years: [Succession](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-egypt-succession)):
  Smenkhkare and Neferneferuaten [C] (1336-1332; kin unknown, perhaps one person) > **Tutankhamun** [A] (1332-1323; son
  of Akhenaten, most think [B]; a boy king steered by Ay and Horemheb [B]) > **Ay** [A] (1323-1320; *official*, the
  God's Father: [14-ay](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-ay)) > **Horemheb** [A] (1319-1292; *official*, the general; a stern restorer who issued an
  edict against corrupt officials and soldiers [A: the Great Edict]) > **Ramesses I** [A] (1292-1291; *official*,
  Horemheb's vizier) > **Seti I** [A] (1290-1279; son; a warrior king, campaigns against the Shasu,
  Qadesh, the Hittites and the Libyans [A]) > **Ramesses II** [A] (1279-1213; son; Qadesh, then the
  peace with Hatti, a great builder [A]) > **Merneptah** [A] (1213-1203; son; beat the Libyans and Sea Peoples [A]) >
  **Seti II** [A] (1200-1194; son; Amenmesse rivalled him [C]). HKW, p. 493.
- <a id="16-line-hatti"></a>**Hatti** ([after him](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-hatti-after)): **Arnuwanda II** [A] (1322-1321; son, the
  heir, who had led armies in Syria [A: *Deeds*]; died of the plague) > **Mursili II** [A] (1321-1295; brother; pious
  and dutiful, the plague prayers; vengeful against Arzawa, whose king mocked him as a child [A]) > **Muwatalli II** [A]
  (1295-1272; son; moved the capital to Tarhuntassa, fought Qadesh) > **Mursili III (Urhi-Teshub)** [A] (1272-1267;
  son, of a second-rank wife) > **Hattusili III** [A] (1267-1237; uncle, *usurper*, who wrote an Apology for it; the
  peace with Egypt, 1259) > **Tudhaliya IV** [A] (1237-1209; son; conquered Alashiya; Kurunta may have held Hattusa
  against him [C]) > **Arnuwanda III** [A] (1209-1207; son) > **Suppiluliuma II** [A] (1207-; brother; the last
  known). Bryce, Kingdom, chronology.
- <a id="16-line-babylonia"></a>**Babylonia**: **Kara-hardash** [A, C his kin] (1333; son or grandson, killed by the
  army) > **Nazi-Bugash** [A] (1333; *usurper*, "son of nobody"; see [14-nazi-bugash](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-nazi-bugash)) > **Kurigalzu
  II** [A] (1332-1308; son of Burna-Buriash by the Synchronistic History [C]; set up by Ashur-uballit; warlike, beat
  Elam and fought Assyria [A: Chronicle P, ABC 21]) > **Nazi-Maruttash** [A] (1307-1282; son; lost to Adad-nirari I)
  > **Kadashman-Turgu** [A] (1281-1264; son) > **Kadashman-Enlil II** [A] (1263-1255; son; a boy whose vizier ruled
  for him, Hattusili III complains [A: KBo I 10]) > **Kudur-Enlil** [A] (1254-1246; kin unclear [C]) >
  **Shagarakti-Shuriash** [A] (1245-1233; son) > **Kashtiliash IV** [A] (1232-1225; son; taken captive by
  Tukulti-Ninurta I). Brinkman 1976.
- <a id="16-line-mitanni"></a>**Mitanni**: the named heir is **Shattiwaza** [A] (son; fled, returned with a Hittite
  army under Piyassili and ruled as Hatti's vassal from about 1330; an exile who won back a throne abroad [A]: see
  [14-shattiwaza](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-shattiwaza)). Between his father's murder and his return the record has the rival line,
  **Artatama II**'s son **Shuttarna III** [A] (*usurper*; bought Assyria's favour with the palace treasure and the
  nobles: [14-artatama](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-artatama)). After Shattiwaza, kings of Hanigalbat known from Assyria's side: **Shattuara
  I** [A, C his kin] (about 1300; rebelled, was taken and made a vassal by Adad-nirari I) > **Wasashatta** [A] (son;
  rebelled again and sought Hatti's help, which "took his bribe" and never came [A]) > **Shattuara II** [A, C his kin]
  (beaten by Shalmaneser I, about 1260; Hanigalbat annexed), then none. Grayson, RIMA 1 (A.0.76.1, 3; A.0.77.1).
- <a id="16-line-assyria"></a>**Assyria** (each the son of the one before, except where marked [A: the Assyrian King List]):
  **Enlil-nirari** [A] (1327-1318; fought Kurigalzu II) > **Arik-den-ili** [A] (1317-1306; campaigned in the Zagros
  and against the Ahlamu) > **Adad-nirari I** [A] (1305-1274; warlike: beat Nazi-Maruttash and crushed Hanigalbat) >
  **Shalmaneser I** [A] (1273-1244; annexed Hanigalbat; boasts of blinding captives [A]) > **Tukulti-Ninurta I** [A]
  (1243-1207; sacked Babylon, murdered by a son [A]) > **Ashur-nadin-apli** [A] (1206-1203; son,
  the father's murderer, most read [B]) > **Ashur-nirari III** [A] (1202-1197; son) > **Enlil-kudurri-usur** [A]
  (1196-1192; uncle, Tukulti-Ninurta's son) > **Ninurta-apil-Ekur** [A] (1191-1179; *usurper* of a side line). Grayson,
  RIMA 1; dates carry about ten years of play.
- <a id="16-line-arzawa"></a>**Arzawa**: after Tarhundaradu a gap [S], then **Uhhaziti** [A] (about 1320-1318; kin
  unknown; defied Mursili II, took in Hatti's fugitives and leaned on Ahhiyawa; fled over the sea and died there [A:
  Annals; [06-uhhaziti](06-attested-people.md#06-uhhaziti)]) > **Piyama-Kurunta** [A] (son; beaten at the Astarpa),
  then none: Mursili II broke Arzawa up among Mira, Seha and Hapalla [A].
- <a id="16-line-alashiya"></a>**Alashiya**: unnamed [S] until **Kushmeshusha** [A] (late 13th century; kin unknown:
  [06-kushmeshusha](06-attested-people.md#06-kushmeshusha)).

### The lands of western Anatolia and the north

- <a id="16-line-seha"></a>**Seha River Land**: **Muwawalwi** [A, C that he ruled in 1346] > **Ura-Hattusa** [A, C
  that he ruled] (son; with his brothers drove out Manapa-Tarhunta) > **Manapa-Tarhunta** [A] (brother; took
  Uhhaziti's side, then was spared when his mother pleaded with Mursili II [A: Annals]; a wavering survivor) >
  **Masturi** [A] (son; Muwatalli II's brother-in-law, who went over to Hattusili III) > **Tarhunaradu** [A] (*usurper*,
  backed by Ahhiyawa against Tudhaliya IV, deposed), then a man of Muwawalwi's line, unnamed [A]. Beckman no. 12; Bryce,
  Kingdom.
- <a id="16-line-mira"></a>**Mira**: unnamed [S] > **Mashhuiluwa** [A] (put in by Mursili II; Suppiluliuma's
  son-in-law, who later rebelled: [14-mashhuiluwa](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-mashhuiluwa)) > **Kupanta-Kurunta** [A] (nephew and adopted son;
  Beckman no. 11) > **Alantalli** [A, C his kin] > **Tarkasnawa** [A] (son; the Karabel relief). Hawkins, *AnSt* 48
  (1998).
- <a id="16-line-azzi"></a>**Azzi-Hayasa**: Hukkana > **Anniya** [A, C his kin] (fought Mursili II for years and
  would not give back Hatti's subjects [A: Annals]: [01-hukkana](01-politics.md#01-hukkana)), then [S].
- <a id="16-line-isuwa"></a>**Isuwa**: unnamed [S]; 13th-century texts name vassal kings Ali-Sharruma, Ari-Sharruma
  (a seal) and Ehli-Sharruma (a letter) [A the names, C order and kin]. Glocker, *AoF* 38 (2011).
- <a id="16-line-chiefs"></a>**Kaska, Lukka, Suteans, Shasu, Tjehenu**: chiefs, no line of succession on record [S];
  later chiefs (Pihhuniya of the Kaska under Mursili II; Meryey of Libya, [01-libya](01-politics.md#01-libya)) are not
  heirs.

### Syria and the Euphrates

- <a id="16-line-carchemish"></a>**Carchemish**: unnamed > **Piyassili (Sharri-Kushuh)** [A] (about 1327-1312;
  *conqueror*, Suppiluliuma's son, viceroy; a general, Mursili II's right hand in Syria [A]) > **Shahurunuwa** [A] (son)
  > **Ini-Teshub** [A] (son; Hatti's judge for Syria) > **Talmi-Teshub** [A] (son) > **Kuzi-Teshub** [A] (son; outlived
  the Hittite empire). Bryce, Kingdom.
- <a id="16-line-aleppo"></a>**Aleppo**: unnamed > **Telipinu** [A] (*conqueror*, Suppiluliuma's son, "the priest") >
  **Talmi-Sharruma** [A] (son; Beckman no. 14) > **Halpa-ziti** [B] (son), then [S].
- <a id="16-line-ugarit"></a>**Ugarit**: **Arhalba** [A] (about 1315-1313; son; soon put aside) > **Niqmepa** [A]
  (1313-1260; brother; Beckman no. 9) > **Ammistamru II** [A] (1260-1235; son; sent back his Amorite wife for "the
  great sin" [A]) > **Ibiranu** [A] (1235-1225; son; rebuked by Hatti for withholding homage [A]) >
  **Niqmaddu III** [A] (1225-1215; son) > **Ammurapi** [A] (1215-1185; kin unclear [C]; the last). Singer 1999.
- <a id="16-line-amurru"></a>**Amurru**: **DU-Teshub** [A] (son) > **Duppi-Teshub** [A] (son; Beckman no. 8) >
  **Bentešina** [A] (son; deposed by Muwatalli II for siding with Egypt, then restored by his friend Hattusili III;
  a survivor [A]) with **Shapili** [A] (*usurper*, set in his place between [C his kin]) > **Shaushga-muwa** [A] (son;
  Beckman no. 17). Singer 1991; Aziru himself: [14-aziru](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-aziru).
- <a id="16-line-qadesh"></a>**Qadesh**: Aitakama > **Niqmaddu** [A] (about 1312; son, who killed his father in a
  revolt against Mursili II and submitted [A: Annals; [Wikipedia, "Etakkama"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etakkama)]), then [S].
- <a id="16-line-nuhashshe"></a>**Nuhashshe**: Addu-nirari > **Tette** [A, C his kin] (Beckman no. 7; rebelled
  against Mursili II [A]), then [S].
- <a id="16-line-niya"></a>**Niya**: Takuwa > **Akit-Teshup** [A] (brother, who turned the town against him:
  [14-niya](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-niya)), then [S].
- <a id="16-line-tunip"></a>**Tunip**: its elders ask for "the son of Aki-Teshup" as their king (EA 59) [A]: the named
  heir, unnamed himself; then [S].
- <a id="16-line-terqa"></a>**Hana (Terqa)**: the late kings of Hana are known but their order is not fixed [C], and
  none is dated after 1346 [S]. Podany 2002.
- <a id="16-line-arrapha"></a>**Arrapha**: the king of 1346 may be Ithi-Teshup, son of Kipi-Teshup [C: the Nuzi
  texts]; no successor [S]; Nuzi burned and Arrapha passed to Assyria by about 1330 [C]. Maidman 2010.
- <a id="16-line-elam"></a>**Elam** (the Igihalkids; order and dates [C]): perhaps **Humban-numena** in 1346 >
  **Untash-Napirisha** (son; a pious builder, the ziggurat of Chogha Zanbil [A]) > **Unpahash-Napirisha** (cousin) >
  the Kidin-Hutrans, the last of whom raided Babylonia [A: Chronicle P]. Chronicle P's "Hurpatila, king of Elam", beaten
  by Kurigalzu II [A], fits no list [C]. Potts 1999.
- <a id="16-line-qatna"></a>**Qatna**: Akizzi, then none: its palace burned in the Syrian war [B].

### The Levant and Canaan

- <a id="16-line-byblos"></a>**Byblos**: Rib-Hadda > **Ilirabih** [B] (younger brother, who shut him out:
  [14-byblos](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-byblos)), then [S].
- <a id="16-line-shechem"></a>**Shechem**: Labaya > his sons [A] (the only named, **Mut-Bahlu**, holds Pella:
  [14-labaya](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-labaya)), then [S].
- <a id="16-line-akko"></a>**Akko**: Surata > **Zatatna** [A] (son; EA 8, 233-235), then [S].
- <a id="16-line-gezer"></a>**Gezer**: Milkilu > **Yapahu** [A] ([06-yapahu](06-attested-people.md#06-yapahu)) >
  **Ba'lu-shipti** [A] (EA 292-294); kin unknown, order [C], then [S].
- <a id="16-line-lachish"></a>**Lachish**: Zimreddi (killed: [14-lachish](14-courts-and-free-cities.md#14-lachish)) > **Shipti-Ba'lu** [A] (EA
  330-332) and **Yabni-ilu** [A] (EA 328); kin unknown, order [C], then [S].
- <a id="16-line-silent"></a>**No successor on record** [S]: Alalakh (Itur-Addu), Arwad (unnamed), Beirut (Ammunira), Pella (Mut-Bahlu), Ashtaroth (Ayyab),
  Sidon (Zimrida), Damascus (Biryawaza), Tyre (Abi-Milku), Megiddo (Biridiya), Jerusalem (Abdi-Heba), Hazor
  (Abdi-Tirshi), Ashkelon (Yidya), Gath (Shuwardata), Aijalon (Ba'lat-nese).
