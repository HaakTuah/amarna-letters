# For a session of another tool

Read at the start of every task by a session of every tool but `claude` (whose part is `tools/claude.md`).

- The project's `AGENTS.md` and each file it names apply to you in full; its `@` lines are Claude Code's way of
  pulling a file in, so open each yourself. Your tool's part: `tasks/tools.md`.
- Handed a brief by another session: build only what it (the PR's description) gives you, and read only what it and
  `AGENTS.md` name. Your work goes to the task's branch, in its PR, which you open if none was (through a plug-in, your
  briefer pushes it); its reviews and merge are your briefer's.
- Started by the owner, with no briefer: you are the task's builder, and finish it as `roles/shared/work-flow.md` says.
- **The tests you are given are the contract:** make them pass; never change, skip or weaken them. If one seems wrong,
  say why in your report and leave that part.
- Run the *other builders' checks* before you finish.
- Report anything missing that the suites need; never work around it.
- List your readings, ideas and bugs in your report, and any helpers you ran with what each gave.
