the archive against today's rules, with five fresh readers in parallel, and the cut-down's commit (d914ffb) line by
line. The cut-down's report listed what it cut, and the owner approved that list (core archive 134); it also said that
every point a reviewer found dropped was put back. Besides "other providers first" (back since #84), six lines resting
on his words had gone without being named. They come back here, said briefly:

when the fallback reviewer takes Claude's PRs (`modules/reviews.md`, point 2); `modules/reviews.md` points to point 3
for when the security review runs, and to floor rule 8 without restating it. The floor and modules are 45,015 bytes of
the 45,056 cap.
