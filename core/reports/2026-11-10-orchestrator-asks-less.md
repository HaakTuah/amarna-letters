# The orchestrator asks less

Both games' orchestrators asked the owner questions whose answer was plainly yes: whether to take over two PRs whose
sessions, started on another account, they could not reach, and whether a PR still waited on an answer he had already
given (core archive 133).

- **`modules/orchestrating.md`, a lane whose session is gone:** a lane whose session the orchestrator cannot reach,
  with no push to its branch for a few hours, goes to a fresh builder from its branch, without asking. The hours
  without a push keep it from doubling a session still alive elsewhere.
- **`modules/orchestrating.md`, ask the owner only what is open:** what the rules, his past words or the stand-in
  settle, the orchestrator does and reports in a line. It asks him only what they leave open, and for a yes that floor
  rules 3 and 14 need.

What changes in every project: an orchestrator takes over a stranded lane by itself, and asks the owner less.

Readings taken: none. Questions for the owner: none.
