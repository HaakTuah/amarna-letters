# The stand-in does more, in the answers it already gives

The owner asked why his stand-in never answers on ideas, and whether it could do more (core archive 142). It already
searches his words for each open point it answers, so its new work rides on that same search instead of calls of its
own. All of it is in `modules/inbox.md`.

What changes in every project:

- **Sure or unsure:** each answer it gives is marked sure or unsure. The unsure lead "Readings taken" in the *report*,
  and are named when the owner is told of the merge, so he reads a few lines instead of every report.
- **Ideas on the same topic:** from the same search it gives its verdict on any open idea on that topic, posted on the
  idea. It closes an idea that goes against his decided word, saying why. A yes stays a verdict: nothing is built from an
  idea until he moves it into the *progress file*, as before.
- **A weekly sweep:** once a week the orchestrator has it sweep, in one call, the ideas no answer has touched.
- **What players will see:** called when such a PR is marked ready, it gives the notes he would likely give its pictures
  and text, so they are fixed before he sees them.
- **Learning from corrections:** it also searches the reports that quote his corrections, so a miss is not repeated.

Not changed: before an orchestrator reports anything as waiting on the owner, it already checks GitHub
(`modules/orchestrating.md`, core archive 137); the owner had seen two such reports that were out of date, which was
that rule not being followed, not a gap in it.

To stay under the cap (`README.md`), text that said again what another file says goes: `modules/inbox.md` pointed to
floor rule 14 and also copied its list of yeses, and restated floor rule 4 on comments from the owner's account; each is
now a pointer. `modules/helpers.md`'s opening line and `modules/github-calls.md`'s command list (aligning spaces, and a
note on what `<repo>` means) are shorter. No rule in them changes.

Readings taken:

- The stand-in is read-only, so "posted on it" and "it closes" are done by the session that called it, as with every
  answer it gives.
- The weekly sweep belongs to the orchestrator, which already wakes every hour.

Questions for the owner: none.
