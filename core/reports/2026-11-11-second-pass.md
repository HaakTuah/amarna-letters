# A second pass over the stand-in and the rules the games repeat

The owner asked whether the stand-in now does everything offered for it (core archive 142), and whether the core and
both games are cohesive, shared and free of duplication. This is the core's part of the answer; each game's goes in a PR
of its own.

What changes in every project:

- **The stand-in's verdict on an idea** says yes, later or no, with the reason and a rough cost, as offered
  (`modules/inbox.md`).
- **A call of taste** (a name, music, a look) is always marked unsure, so it leads "Readings taken" and is named when
  the owner is told of the merge, as the offered weekly list of taste calls would have.
- **The stand-in's notes on a PR players will see** are listed among the reviews a PR gets (`modules/reviews.md`,
  "Which"), where a builder marking a PR ready looks; `modules/inbox.md` says only what they are.
- **The weekly sweep of ideas** moves from `modules/inbox.md` into the orchestrator's wake (`modules/orchestrating.md`),
  the list of what it does when it wakes.
- **A blocked task gives way to the next that does not depend on it** (`modules/how-to-work.md`, "Don't loop"): Amarna
  Letters' builder rules said it, and lost it when they were cut down to what is that game's own (its #907, the
  fallback reviewer's one point).
- **A reading says what it rests on** (`modules/how-to-work.md`, "The work never waits"; core archive 144): Amarna
  Letters' design document asked it of that game's builders; it holds for every project, so it moves here.

Text that said the same thing twice goes, and no rule changes: that only the yeses floor rule 14 names wait for the
owner stood in floor rule 14 and again in `modules/how-to-work.md`, `modules/inbox.md` and `modules/orchestrating.md`;
each is now a pointer to the floor, and `modules/inbox.md` keeps only what is its own.

Readings taken, the unsure first:

- Unsure: "A reading says what it rests on" was not among the four rules named when the owner gave his yes in core
  archive 143; it is the same kind (a rule one game wrote that holds for every project), and the audit he asked for
  found it, so it moves under that yes. His words behind it come with it (core archive 144).
- The stand-in's check of claims that something waits on the owner, also offered, was already the orchestrator's
  (`modules/orchestrating.md`, core archive 137), with the same effect; it stays there.
- The stand-in is read-only, so a verdict "posted" and an idea "closed" are the calling session's acts, as before.

Questions for the owner: none.
