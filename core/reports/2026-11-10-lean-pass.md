# The lean pass (10 November 2026)

Every file of the core, the scripts, workflows and templates included, read line by line against five questions: who
reads it and what would they do wrong without it; is it said elsewhere; is it a rule, or explanation, history, a
repeated reason or an example; is it where its readers look; does anything use it. The owner's standard: no useless fat
(core archive 95). Cut words, not protection: each cut below is a word, a repeat, an explanation or a pointer no reader
needs, or a rule wholly said elsewhere, named beside it. Both games were read on `develop` (Amarna Letters at
`8b460a0`, Barkstreet Snakes at `01a54d7`); nothing was written to either. Before: `main` at `0fa20e1`.

Two PRs: the floor, `modules/review-guidelines.md` and `.github/` need the owner's yes, so they are a PR of their own
(B); the rest (A) can merge without waiting.
