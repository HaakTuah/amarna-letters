# The six lines go again

`reports/2026-11-11-silent-cuts-restored.md` put back six lines the cut-down of 10 November had removed without naming
them. The owner, the same evening: "Do we actually need any of this stuff. I think the cutter were spot on on msot of
these, except about removing the use other providers first. Your expert take?"

Judged by what they say rather than by whether the cut-down listed them, none is needed, and they go again:

- **Splitting with a purpose:** "one thing a file, as small as that thing is" already sets the floor, and "one fact,
  one place" already writes a family's shared part once.
- **Context for everything put before him:** the owner's remark was to the design session, which keeps it; issues still
  say in plain words what they are and why they matter.
- **The design check on Sonnet's PRs:** Sonnet takes the routine lanes; it hands what needs judgement to Opus, Codex
  reviews its code, and a PR players will see gets the stand-in's notes.
- **Who merges Dependabot's PRs:** updates are upkeep that waits for no queue entry, and floor rule 17 sets when one
  merges.
- **When a security review counts as run:** Codex's summary shows it, and floor rule 8 already holds the merge until it
  has.
- **Ideas closing once he decides:** the stand-in closes one against his decided word, and one he takes up is closed
  by the work that builds it.

"Other providers first" stays (#84). The three places that stopped restating others to make room stay as they are:
`modules/roles.md` and `modules/reviews.md` point to `modules/reviews.md`, point 2 and 3, and floor rule 8 instead of
repeating them. The floor and modules are 44,378 bytes of the 45,056 cap.
