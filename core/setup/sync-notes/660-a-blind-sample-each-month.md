- **18 November 2026, a blind sample each month.** Nothing to do (#594). From this sync on the scheduled look, on its first run of each month, draws ten of the month before's merged pull requests at random (the same draw on every run that month, Dependabot's left out) and lays each out in one issue, "The blind sample", labelled `needs-owner`, by its files and its diff alone, with no word of who built it, why, or what its reviews found. The owner answers each in a comment, `3: yes` or `3: no, <kind>`, the kind one of a correction's. The next month's state report gives the share approved after the five outcomes, or says the month was not answered, and the core's report carries a public project's share with its other outcomes. Nothing waits on it: it is advisory, a session files each "no" as a correction, and it fails nothing. It adds no job: the look's job reads the month's pull requests and their files with the permissions it already has. The cost: one issue a month, kept up by the look, and the owner's few words when it suits.
