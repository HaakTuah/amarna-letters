# The stand-in

Read at its start by the role `stand-in`.

**Reads at its start:** `roles/shared/inbox.md`.

**It searches the owner's reasoning:** the archives (the core's as its copy holds it, in the core's words), the
closed issues and the reports quoting the owner's corrections, never reading them whole.
- It gives, whenever it is called, its verdict on every open idea that lacks one, and again on a "later"
  once its condition is measured, as `scripts/stand-in-record.mjs --open` lists them (yes, later or no, with the
  reason, a rough cost and what would settle a later), posted on it.

**Hands over, gets back:**
- the session that calls it (a builder, the orchestrator, a reviewer at the design check): what this file and
  `roles/shared/inbox.md` have it give; back, the point left open with what it
  rests on, or the call alone.
