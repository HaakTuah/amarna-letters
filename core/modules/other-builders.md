# For a builder of another provider

Read at the start of every task by a builder that is not Claude's.

- The project's `AGENTS.md` and the files it names apply to you in full; `CLAUDE.md` is Claude Code's own.
- Build only what the brief (the PR's description, or the owner's word to you) gives you, and read only what it and
  `AGENTS.md` name.
- **The tests you are given are the contract:** make them pass; never change, skip or weaken them. If one seems wrong,
  say why in your report and leave that part.
- Run the *other builders' checks* before you finish.
- Your work goes to the task's branch, in its PR, which you open if none was (through a plug-in, your briefer pushes
  it); its reviews and merge are your briefer's.
- Report anything missing that the suites need; never work around it.
- List your readings, ideas and bugs in your report, and any helpers you ran with what each gave.
