# The reasons behind the core: issue #887

The index, and how an item is written: `archive/instructions.md`.

#887. **19 November 2026**, #887. Whoever writes or changes a rule of the floor, the modules or the core's `AGENTS.md`
     looks first for a form of it, the rule as asked or one slightly different, whose breach a check reads without
     guessing, writes that form and names the check; the form only a reading holds is kept only where the checkable one
     would change what the rule asks or protects a great deal, and the pull request says why. The design check blocks a
     rule held by reading alone where a checkable form exists and no such reason is given, a project's design checks
     reading its own rules the same way from the release that brings it, and each pruning proposes,
     for each rule with no holder, beside its other proposal, a slightly different form a check could hold, or why none
     exists. It does not mean that a rule's purpose is bent to fit a check, nor that a check that guesses is written. A
     rule a check holds need not be given to a session ahead of its work once release 7 (#692) begins, and is kept
     whether or not it is read, while one held by reading costs every session that reads it. The owner gave it as a
     standard for every rule while agreeing to #886, and agreed to it as the issue sets it out, a standing rule. It
     stands behind "A rule is written in the form a check can hold" and the sixth part of "A rule is written to one
     pattern" in `AGENTS.md`, its point under "A rule file changed" in `roles/shared/review-guidelines.md`, and the
     forms of the pruning's list in `scripts/pruning.mjs` and `reports/unheld.json`.
