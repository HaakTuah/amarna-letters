# The reasons behind the core: issue #593.8

The index, and how an item is written: `archive/instructions.md`.

#593.8. **17 November 2026**, #593, points 8 and 9. A part whose bugs repeat one kind, an input, a project or a platform
     it did not read, is read once for its whole input at the third such bug, counted from the issues labelled `bug`
     that name the part's files: its builder lists every kind its job covers from the source that defines them and
     gives each a test, or files the tests as a task; and where something outside defines the full set, the part's test
     runs the part and that authority on the same inputs and fails on any difference, since kinds listed by hand are as
     complete as their writer's memory. The owner agreed to both, with the issue's other points, after asking what the
     core lacked that let a check cover only the cases its writer thought of, which 30 of its 58 bugs by that day had
     been; on 18 November 2026 the owner agreed that point 8 reads for any project, its count no longer leaning on the
     core's inventory. It stands behind "A part whose gaps repeat is read for its whole input" in `tasks/code-shape.md`.
