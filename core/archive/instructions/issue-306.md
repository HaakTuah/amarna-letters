#306. **15 November 2026**, #306. A session runs the project's quick check before its first change, and a failure there
     is a bug of its own, filed and not folded into its task; a session that stops mid-task, at a passed gate, a limit
     or the stop switch, leaves a hand-off note of a fixed form, what is done, what was tried and failed, what is next
     and how to check it, which the builder that takes over its lane reads first: a session that starts on a broken
     `main` cannot tell its own faults from those it inherited, and a lane handed over from its branch alone shows none
     of what was tried and failed, so it is tried again. The owner agreed to it among the additions a session set out
     after checking the core's rules against the field's practice, the owner having asked what mistakes the process
     does not prompt the models to avoid or detect. It stands behind "Sessions" in `roles/shared/work-flow.md`, "A
     change of tool loses nothing" in `tasks/tools.md`, "A stalled lane" and "A finished session is archived" in
     `roles/orchestrator.md`, the *quick check*, the start hook's line and `scripts/stalled.mjs`'s mark.
