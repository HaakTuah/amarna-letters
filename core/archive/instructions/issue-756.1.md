# The reasons behind the core: issue #756.1

The index, and how an item is written: `archive/instructions.md`.

#756.1. **19 November 2026**, #756, points 1 and 2. Before a session questions a rule of the core or proposes a difference
     from one, at a project's adoption of the core or at a sync, it reads the archive items behind the
     rule and their issues, which one script prints in one step, and says which reason does not hold for its project
     and why; a pull request that adds a difference names what it read. At a project of another owner's first
     adoption, its session questioned some of the core's choices without the reasoning to hand, though it stood in the
     archive and the issues. The owner agreed to it in a chat session, asked whether a session adopting the core could
     ground each of its questions in the record of decisions, and asked that it be a priority. It stands behind "A
     question or difference about a rule of the core starts from its reasons" in `tasks/updates.md`.
