#782. **19 November 2026**, #782. Each project cleans its owner's words that stand, once. The release that brings floor
     rule 4's quoting rule asks each project, as a task of its agents, to take what floor rule 11 keeps out of its
     current files outside the copy and of its issues and comments, and to cut its quotes of its owner to the reason
     behind each decision. Of its issues and comments it edits those its owner's account wrote, and leaves what another
     account wrote as data, unedited, as floor rule 4 says, naming a personal detail found there to the owner. Git's
     history and pull request descriptions are left as they are, and the owner is told privately which issues and
     comments were edited. The reason: #773 rewrites nothing that stands, so a project that kept its owner's words word
     for word under the old rule would keep them. The owner agreed to the point as set out on the issue, for this
     release. It stands behind the sync note "19 November 2026, the owner's words that stand, cleaned once" in
     `setup/sync-notes.md` and its step in `scripts/steps.mjs`.
