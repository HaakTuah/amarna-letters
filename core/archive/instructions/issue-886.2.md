# The reasons behind the core: issue #886.2

The index, and how an item is written: `archive/instructions.md`.

#886.2. **19 November 2026**, #886, point 2. The owner's yes, for a change to the floor or the review guidelines and
     for a changed rule joining the queue, is asked only where the change alters what a session must or may do, what
     protects the owner or who decides. A change that keeps the meaning (a path or pointer that follows a move, a
     spelling or grammar mend, text moved or merged unchanged, a name updated) needs none: `scripts/path-only.mjs`
     proves a moved path, and otherwise the design check, by a reviewer who did not write the change, states it on a
     line "Meaning unchanged:" that the description check reads (`scripts/meaning-kept.mjs`); any doubt goes to the
     owner, the session that wrote the change never decides alone, and the monthly state report lists the changes to
     the floor and the review guidelines merged without the yes, so the owner can look them over. It does not mean that
     a new rule, or a change that loosens or tightens one, goes without the yes. A yes asked for what means nothing
     becomes a formality, and the work waits on it all the same; the owner's yes had been asked for a one-word pointer
     and a wording mend that kept its meaning. The owner agreed to the point as the issue sets it out, adding that a
     rule a script proves is the ideal. It stands behind "The owner's yes in the PR", of floor rule 3, in
     `floor/security.md`, "A change that keeps the meaning is stated so" in `roles/shared/review-guidelines.md`, and
     "An idea is built only from a line" in `AGENTS.md`.
     Changes: #886.1.
