# The reasons behind the core: issue #756.3

The index, and how an item is written: `archive/instructions.md`.

#756.3. **19 November 2026**, #756, point 3. An item of the archive that changes what an earlier one decided names it
     on a line "Changes:", and the earlier one gains a line "Changed by:" naming it, its text otherwise unchanged; a
     check holds the two in step, and the script of point 2 prints the line under the item and the later items after
     it. Some items said in passing that they replaced an earlier one while nothing linked the earlier item forward, so
     a session reading it could rely on a reason that no longer stood; twelve such pairs were found among the items
     that stand and marked. The owner agreed to it in a chat session, as part of #756, and asked that it be a priority.
     It stands behind "An item that changes an earlier one names it, and the earlier one is marked" in `AGENTS.md`.
